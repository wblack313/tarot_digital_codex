# The Minor Arcana

The Minor Arcana consists of 56 cards—out of a 78-card tarot deck—that represent daily life, temporary emotions, and specific actions. The minor arcana is divided in four suits: 
- 🔥 [Wands](wands/)
- 🏺 [Cups](cups/)
- ⭐ [Pentacles](pentacles/)
- ⚔️ [Swords](swords/)


Each suit maps to a practical domain of human life: 
- 🐉 **Spirit**, _Lifeforce & Strength_  - [Principle of Fire](../general_notes/four_elements/README.md#fire)  🔥
- ❤️ **Heart**, _Emotions & Relationships_ - [Principle of Water](../general_notes/four_elements/README.md#water) 💧
- ⚔️ **Victory**, _Conquest & Warfare_ - [Principle of Air](../general_notes/four_elements/README.md#earth)  💨
- 💰 **Wealth**, _Resources & Wellbeing_ - [Principle of Earth](../general_notes/four_elements/README.md#earth) 🌱
